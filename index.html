Meet Josh, Clara, Mommy & Daddy

The characters of the Cute Oops picture book series. Every book brings the whole family together for a new oops.

Josh

The ball champion

Josh loves his big red ball. He kicks first and thinks later, and he has a talent for keeping quiet when the oops is his fault.

Clara

The great-ideas girl

Clara always has a GREAT idea, like turning a fallen plant into a beautiful bouquet. She is curious, cuddly and very fond of cake.

Mommy

The calm and kind one

Mommy has a big smile, a big heart and a lot of patience. Even after a flying ball, a plant on the floor and a cake disaster, she keeps smiling.

Daddy

The cleanup hero

Daddy is the king of cleanup and the master of the firm "No more cake!" He always finds the funny side of every oops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about the Josh & Clara picture book series.

What is Josh & Clara – Cute Oops?

It is a funny, heartwarming picture book series about two toddler siblings, Josh and Clara, and the little accidents that turn an ordinary day upside down. Every book is a new "oops" the whole family can laugh about.

Who are Josh and Clara?

Josh and Clara are the author's real children. They tell their dad which "oops" should go into each book, and the stories turn those ideas into funny, warm picture books for the whole family.

What is Book 1, The Ball in the House, about?

A quiet afternoon at home turns chaotic when Josh's ball knocks over Mommy's plant, Clara makes a bouquet from the flowers, and another rolling ball sends a cake flying.

What age is the book for?

The series is written for children aged 2 to 6, with short sentences and expressive illustrations that work well for read-aloud time and for early readers.

Is it a good bedtime story or classroom read-aloud?

Yes. The stories are short, funny and end cozily, which makes them a great fit for bedtime, daycare, preschool and family reading.

About the Series

Josh & Clara are two curious, lovable siblings who turn ordinary days into big adventures, and sometimes into big messes. Each story is made for ages 2 to 6, with simple words, expressive pictures and a cozy, funny ending.

Every "oops" in the series is suggested by the author's own children, Josh and Clara. Perfect for bedtime, daycare, classrooms and family reading.

Meet the Authors

Christophe, Josh & Clara

Hi, I'm Christophe, dad of Josh and Clara, and I live in Geneva, Switzerland. Josh and Clara are my real children, and they are the ones who tell me which "oops" goes into each book. Every story starts with their ideas and their giggles, which is why the accidents feel so real and so funny.

I have also spent more than 12 years building digital products, which taught me how to turn simple ideas into things people love. Josh & Clara – Cute Oops is where that experience meets storytelling. My goal is simple: make children laugh and give parents a book they enjoy reading again and again.
